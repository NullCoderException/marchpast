# Units carry an arm, and every view draws every arm

*Amended 2026-09-06: the chart plate's infantry and cavalry signs and the formation word v2 adds were decided by ADR-0016; a parent of mixed arms carries the arm of its main body (ADR-0017).*

The v1 roster says who a unit is (`id`, `side`, `label`, `commander`) and nothing about what it is made of, so the renderer draws the same ship-ticks for Hasdrubal's horse, the Libyan foot and Nelson's column; only the label tells them apart. The Cannae research ([#41](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/41)) lists that first among the things v1 cannot express. We decided that every roster unit carries a required **`arm`**, one word from a flat, closed, code-extensible list that v2 ships with exactly the values the four battles use: `infantry`, `cavalry` and `ship`. The arm is part of a unit's identity, like its commander, and never changes during a battle. It reaches the drawing as one field on the glyph request, and **every view draws every arm** with its own sign, enforced by the type so that a new arm cannot compile until each view has drawn it. The legend keys arms only when a battle has more than one. Trekroner stays a work on the map, not a unit, so no `battery` arm ships in v2.

## Considered options

- **Arm as per-phase state.** Rejected: no battle on this map has a unit whose composition changes mid-battle. Cavalry that dismounts, or a squadron that lands its marines, is two units or caption matter, the way a change of command is (ADR-0010).
- **Calling the field `type`.** The ticket's word, rejected: `type` is already GeoJSON's discriminator in every map file and a keyword in every source file, so the glossary and the code would both be saying two things with one word.
- **Optional, absent meaning `ship`.** Rejected: it hides the naval default in the file and leaves the validator unable to catch a forgotten arm on a Cannae wing. Schema v2 already obliges Trafalgar's file to change (`summary`, `dates`, `sort_date`), so a required field costs three edits there.
- **An arm plus a qualifier** (heavy or light infantry, heavy cavalry or skirmishers, ship of the line or frigate). Rejected: the one place it would earn its keep on this map is Numidian light horse against Hasdrubal's heavy horse, and the velites and slingers are already folded into the opening skirmish rather than being units. A qualifier multiplies the signs every view must draw, for a distinction the label already carries ("Numidian horse").
- **A `battery` arm, making Trekroner a unit** in the Copenhagen file. Rejected: a battery unit would need a heading and a formation, which the schema requires of every snapshot, and its glyph would sit on the map's own bastioned sign at the same coordinates. The Copenhagen research recommends folding its fire into the Danish line's caption, and ADR-0012 already draws it as a `work`.
- **A glyph per arm**: each view supplies separate glyphs for ships, foot and horse. Rejected in favour of one glyph per view whose body varies by arm, because the glyph's two halves (ADR-0014), its formation handling and its label clearance are the same whatever the arm; only the repeated shape differs.
- **A fallback sign** for an arm a view has not drawn. Rejected: a view that cannot tell infantry from cavalry is a broken view, not a degraded one.
- **Always keying arms in the legend.** Rejected: Trafalgar's legend is already the tallest piece of furniture and would gain a row that says "ship" under three units that are visibly ships.
- **A `sides` block**, deferred to this decision by ADR-0011. Rejected for v2: the derived order (first appearance in the roster) already puts Nelson first in every naval file and Rome first at Cannae, and nothing on this map gives a side anything else to hold. Hierarchy may reopen it.

## Consequences

- **Schema v2 adds `arm` to the roster unit, required.** Its values are a code-level allowlist like the licence identifiers (ADR-0007): a missing or unknown arm is a validation error, never ignored, and adding an arm is a code change that adds the value and a sign in every view. Trafalgar's three units gain `arm: "ship"`; the Nile's and Copenhagen's units are all `ship`; Cannae's wings are `infantry` or `cavalry`.
- **The glyph request carries the arm, and a glyph carries a sign per arm.** The sign is the repeated shape a view draws one arm in: on the chart plate and the night plate it is the v1 chevron tick for a ship, unchanged. The table is keyed by arm, so the compiler, not a reviewer, notices the view that forgot one.
- **Atlas keeps one block for every arm and puts a sign inside it:** a single diagonal across the block for cavalry, crossed diagonals for infantry, plain for a ship. These are independent of formation, and they are the convention of nineteenth-century battle atlases and of modern military map symbols alike, so a reader brings them to the plate.
- **The chart plate's infantry and cavalry signs are decided by the land-units decision** ([#49](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/49)), together with the formation words v2 adds, because a rank of foot and a troop of horse are shaped by their formation in a way a ship's tick is not. Whether a land unit's engaged mark is the plate's smoke or something else is that decision's too. This decision fixes only that there is one sign per arm per view and where it plugs in.
- **The legend keys arms only when the roster has two or more,** one row per arm present, each sampled from the view's own glyph the way the state rows are. Trafalgar's legend is unchanged; Cannae's gains two rows. On a phone the arm rows go to Details with the state rows.
- **Heavy and light, ships of the line and frigates, stay label and caption matter.** The Nile's optional frigate unit is a `ship`.
- **A parent unit of mixed arms** (an army of foot and horse) is the hierarchy decision's problem ([#50](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/50)); the vocabulary carries no `mixed` value and this decision does not reserve one.
- **`artillery`, `armour`, `air` and `battery` are not reserved words.** Each arrives additively, with its signs, when a battle needs it; naming them now would be schema no battle exercises.
- **Sides stay derived from the units.** ADR-0011's derivation stands as the only mechanism.
