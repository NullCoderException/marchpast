# State, strength and moves carry Cannae unchanged, with `broken` widened and a returning detachment costing nothing

*Amended 2026-09-07: "Copenhagen's truce and the anchored lines are still open" was closed by [ADR-0019](0019-anchoring-and-the-truce-are-caption-matter-and-heading-is-the-fighting-front.md) — both are caption matter and heading is the fighting front. The rejected option of following a wing off the extent so it blinks back stays rejected on [ADR-0024](0024-aircraft-are-an-arm-and-a-unit-may-be-absent-from-a-phase.md)'s own terms: absence means a unit does not exist on the plate, never that it is off the extent, and a unit never reappears. Everything else here stands.*

*Amended 2026-09-06: `docs/schema.md` is now the v2 spec, written at the handoff (#53).*

ADR-0003 fixed a four-value **state** enum and an optional **strength** fraction; ADR-0004 fixed two **move** kinds and made a unit's own motion the renderer's **track**, with no schema field; ADR-0016 gave land units their signs and let a track run behind a unit that keeps its heading. Cannae is the first land battle authored against all three ([#41](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/41)), and it is the hardest case v0.2 has: legions that fight on in fragments until they are killed where they stand, a cavalry wing that rides off the field in pursuit and comes back, a wing that flees with almost all its men alive, an army whose losses no source counts as they mount, and a ride round the enemy's rear that ends facing the way it came.

We decided that **v1's vocabulary holds**: four states, one fraction, two move kinds, no waypoints and no turn hint. Two things sharpen. **`broken` means no longer acting as one body**, whether the unit flees, as Gravina's remnant did, or fights on in fragments, as the legions did; and **a detachment reduces strength only when the detached part does not return to the unit**, so a pursuit that comes back is an arrow and costs nothing.

## Considered options

- **A fifth state, `routed` beside `broken`**, splitting flight from fighting on in fragments. Rejected: a fifth glyph, a fifth legend row and a distinction the sources do not draw consistently, to separate two things one caption sentence already separates.
- **Calling the encircled legions `engaged` to the end**, on the grounds that they never stopped fighting. Rejected: it throws away the one moment the state field exists to show, and Polybius is explicit that the line is gone — they fought "no longer in line, yet singly, or in maniples" (III.115).
- **A state for a unit that has left the field** (dispersed in pursuit, off the plate). Rejected: one enum would then carry both a condition and a location, and the legend would key a "state" that is neither. State answers what condition a unit is in; position answers where it is.
- **Following the pursuing wing off the extent with its position**, so the plate loses the Numidians for two phases and they blink back. Rejected: a unit vanishing and reappearing mid-battle is what ADR-0003 deferred, arriving by the back door and without the schema noticing.
- **A schema flag marking a strength as estimated.** Rejected: at Cannae it would be true of nearly every field, `t` included, since the only source-pinned instant of the day is sunrise. The author's reasoning belongs in the phase's `notes`, which ADR-0006 provides for exactly this.
- **An absolute count of men** for a land battle, revisiting ADR-0003. Rejected on its original grounds and on a new one: the sources give a final total and no running count, so a typed denominator would be as invented as the fraction and harder to read.
- **A third move kind** (`pursuit`, `retreat`, `envelopment`). Rejected: all three cases fall to the two kinds, and each new kind is a line style the legend must key and every view must draw.
- **An `intent` arrow for the feigned withdrawal**, saying the centre meant to give ground. Rejected: it points where the track already goes, which is the duplicated motion ADR-0004 exists to forbid. The plan is Hannibal's and lives in the caption.
- **A per-phase turn-direction hint** beside `heading`, so Hasdrubal wheels back through the north. Rejected: a second number in every snapshot for a cosmetic arc, and `lerpHeading` already rules that the author adds a phase instead.
- **Waypoints on a position**, so a track can bend round a unit it would otherwise cut. Rejected: geometry in data, which ADR-0001 rules out, and it breaks ADR-0002's "the phase _is_ the positions".

## Consequences

- **`broken` covers flight and fragments alike.** The label prints **Broken** beside the encircled legions in phase 7 and beside the fleeing allied horse in phase 6; the caption says which picture it is. A viewer who learned the word at Trafalgar reads it at Cannae as "this is no longer one body", which is what it always meant.
- **A state change inside one phase is a signal to split the phase.** The research table's "engaged then broken" for the Roman infantry is ADR-0003's "an author who wants a gentler collapse adds a phase", and the phase it wants — the circle closing — has plenty to caption.
- **`destroyed` is gone as a fighting unit, not annihilated.** The allied cavalry is `destroyed` in phase 7 with three hundred of its men riding safely to Venusia. This is ADR-0003's battle-neutral word doing the job it was chosen for; what became of the men is caption matter.
- **A state is a condition, never a place.** The Numidian wing keeps its ground on the Roman left and stays `engaged` while its pursuit runs off the eastern edge of the extent as a `detachment` arrow — ADR-0004's "part of the unit going where the unit does not", used as written.
- **Strength was never a casualty count, so "left rather than fallen" is not a special case.** It is the fraction still fighting *as part of the unit*, so men who have run are as gone from it as men who have fallen, and a wing is near zero the moment it breaks, before anyone dies. The allied horse at 0.1 is honest.
- **A detachment costs strength only when the detached part does not return.** Dumanoir's van never came back and reduced the Combined Fleet; the Numidians come back, so they hold at 1 while their arrow appears and is gone. Strength therefore never rises in practice, and no rule forbids a rise: validation stays shape-only (ADR-0004).
- **Strength is an author's reading, held to the standard `t` already is at Cannae**, and a step wants a source event to hang on. A drift of invented fractions between two attested endpoints is the decay curve nobody authored that ADR-0003's no-tween clause exists to prevent, moved from the tween into the phases; the phase's `references` and `notes` carry what the sources actually say.
- **Both move kinds are exercised and neither is stretched.** The Numidian pursuit is a `detachment`; Hasdrubal's ride round the rear is an `intent` at the allied cavalry's position, because the whole unit goes and a detachment would be wrong; the envelopment is the Libyans' own headings and positions, with `intent` arrows at the Roman flanks if the pincer needs to be legible before the positions arrive; the feigned withdrawal is the centre's own motion plus a caption.
- **The shortest-arc heading rule stands, and a steering phase must earn its caption.** `caption` is required, so a phase added only to turn a unit the long way round would be a phase with nothing to say. At Cannae none is needed: Hasdrubal's wheel back through the north falls on phase 6, where he leaves the pursuit to the Numidians, one of the phases the research says must stay separate.
- **A track that cuts another unit's glyph is a position choice, not a schema gap.** The author steers it by where the intermediate phase puts the unit; the renderer routes around nothing, and ADR-0016's rule that the label pass may not assume a track runs ahead is unaffected.
- **Nothing in `src/schema/` changes and no field is added.** This decision is wording and authoring discipline. It amends the glossary's **State** and **Strength** entries; `docs/schema.md` stays the v1 spec until the handoff ([#53](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/53)) writes v2.
- **Copenhagen's truce and the anchored lines are still open.** Whether a fleet at anchor or a ceasefire needs anything the four states lack is the naval question this map still holds in fog; nothing here forecloses it.
