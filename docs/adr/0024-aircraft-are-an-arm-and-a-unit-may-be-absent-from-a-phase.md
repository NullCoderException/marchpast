# Aircraft are an arm, and a unit may be absent from a phase

The Midway research ([#124](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/124)) reports a battle whose story is carrier strikes: VT-8 flying in alone and not coming back, three carriers bombed in six minutes, Hiryu's two counter-strikes on Yorktown. v2 has three arms (`infantry`, `cavalry`, `ship`) and no way to put an aeroplane on the plate, and ADR-0015 declined to reserve one "until a battle needs it". Midway needs it. We decided that **`aircraft` joins the arm allowlist**, and that **a unit may be absent from a phase**, meaning it does not exist on the plate at that instant — the thing ADR-0017 deferred as "units appearing mid-battle". A strike gets a snapshot at its launch, its attack and its recovery and none before or after; the sixteen-unit ceiling is counted per level **per phase**; a strike's `parent` is its force, never its ship. Between them these add one enum value and no field at all.

The second half is not a convenience. Under ADR-0017 a level draws every unit at that depth plus every shallower unit with no children, so a carrier whose strike is airborne **drops off the plate**: Hiryu vanishes at 10:58 and again at 13:31, precisely when she is the last carrier afloat and the only one still fighting. Because every phase had to list every unit, ten strike glyphs also sat on open sea at 04:30 above carriers that were not drawn. Absence deletes both, and it is the same rule Alesia asks for in its own words — the relief army that exists before it appears ([#132](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/132) inherits it and does not re-decide it).

## Considered options

- **Strikes as `intent` and `detachment` arrows, no aircraft units** (the two-level file the research says is writable in v2 today). Rejected, and it is the one rejection that costs something real: it is free, honest and needs no schema at all. It buys a Midway in which no aeroplane is ever drawn, at the battle that is about aeroplanes, which is not a Midway worth adding to the library. The arrows stay available and Hornet's group, which found nothing, is authored as one.
- **A clause drawing a parent whose children are of a different arm.** The research's first candidate, rejected on arithmetic: it puts Hiryu, Hornet, Enterprise, Yorktown and Midway's air group back on the strikes level and takes it from fourteen units to **nineteen**, over the ceiling, so the fix for the hole breaks the rule the hole was dug to respect.
- **An `embarked` state that draws nothing.** The research's second candidate, rejected because it treats the symptom: it hides the strikes parked on empty sea but leaves the hole where Hiryu should be, and it spends a fifth state value — the four are a closed reading of a unit's condition (ADR-0018) — on what is not a condition but an absence.
- **A child unit per carrier standing for the ship herself** (`hiryu-ship` under `hiryu`). Rejected: it doubles the carriers on the roster to nineteen entries for a distinction no source draws, and it says a carrier is part of herself.
- **A strike parented to its carrier.** Rejected as the direct cause of the vanishing parent. A strike is not a part of a ship the way a squadron is part of a column; it is what leaves the ship. The parent is the force, and ships and strikes are siblings at depth 1.
- **Curating the roster to about ten strikes for the whole battle**, the research's own answer to the ceiling. Superseded rather than rejected: per-phase counting makes the constraint "how many are airborne at 07:55", not "how many exist all day", so all sixteen strikes the sources describe become affordable and the trimming is done where the plate is actually crowded.
- **Absence meaning "position unknown" or "outside the extent".** Rejected: that is what clipping is for, and a unit that exists five hundred miles away is authored or left off the roster. Without the narrow reading, absence becomes the excuse for the unauthored snapshot rule 7 exists to catch.
- **Letting a unit reappear after an absence.** Rejected in favour of one contiguous run. Enterprise's group flies at 07:00 and again at 15:30, and those are two units, not one that blinks: absence makes the second free, and each sortie's opening strength is then its own rather than a fraction of a fraction.
- **A fade as a unit appears and goes.** Rejected: a level switch already has no crossfade (ADR-0017), and a fade needs a duration, which is a second kind of animation the file format does not carry. The pop at a launch phase is the launch.
- **`air` rather than `aircraft`.** Rejected: an arm names what a unit is made of — `ship`, not `naval` — and the things are aeroplanes.
- **A fourth formation word for a strike.** Rejected: ADR-0016's list is flat and never cross-checked against the arm, and it already covers what the sources describe — `mass` for the Japanese "three divisional 'V''s of 9" and for the American circular groups, `column` for a torpedo squadron in file, `line` for a division abreast.
- **`destroyed` at the fatal hit** rather than at the sinking. Rejected: Yorktown was bombed at 12:14, had her fires out and was under way at 19 knots by 14:30, so a file that spends `destroyed` on the hit has no word left for the difference between Yorktown then and Yorktown abandoned at 14:55.
- **Bare parchment for the open ocean** (`map` is already optional and means exactly that). Rejected for Midway: the atoll is a dot on a 400-mile plate, but it is the thing every unit is moving toward or away from, and its reef ring and place label are the only scale cue the viewer gets besides the scale bar.

## Consequences

- **`aircraft` joins `ARMS` in `arms.ts`**, additively, exactly as ADR-0015 planned: a value and a sign in every view. The sign table is keyed by `Arm`, so the compiler refuses a view that has not drawn it — the chart plate, the night plate, Atlas **and the staff map** ([#139](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/139)), which is being drawn now. What the sign looks like is [#140](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/140)'s; this decision fixes only that there is one.
- **A phase need not list every roster unit.** Rule 7 becomes: at most one snapshot per roster unit, every id on the roster, and a unit's phases form one contiguous run of at least one phase. This **supersedes ADR-0017's "every roster unit, parent or child, has exactly one snapshot in every phase"** and undefers its "units appearing mid-battle". Existing files list every unit in every phase and stay valid unchanged.
- **Absence means the unit does not exist on the plate at that instant** — not yet launched, already recovered, not yet arrived, gone. Never "unknown" and never "off the extent".
- **A unit is drawn across the interval between two phases only when it has a snapshot at both ends.** It appears at the instant of its first phase and is gone after the instant of its last, with no fade. So a launch phase puts the strike on its carrier and it tweens outward; a recovery phase brings it home and it is struck below. ADR-0002's tween is unchanged: position tweens, existence does not.
- **`unitsAtLevel` stays a function of the roster; presence filters its result.** Which units a level *can* draw is a property of the tree and does not change with the phase, so a parent never pops onto a finer level in the hours its children happen to be away — a parent that must stay visible there is given a permanent childless child, as `midway-air` is. What varies per phase is only which of those units are present.
- **The sixteen ceiling is counted per level per phase**, not over the whole roster: `unitsAtLevel` for the level, intersected with the phase's snapshots. Twelve stays the guidance in `docs/schema.md`.
- **The legend keys arms on the roster at the level shown, not on the arms drawn this phase**, so the aircraft row does not blink out on 6 June when only ships are left. This narrows ADR-0015's "one row per arm present".
- **The muster rebuilds on a phase change**, not merely relabels ([#130](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/130)): the drawn set now changes with the phase. Still never per frame.
- **No new field, and `schema_version` stays `2`.** One enum member and one relaxed rule; every v2 file already written is still valid. Whether Alesia and the Little Bighorn can say the same is [#132](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/132)'s and [#133](https://github.com/NullCoderException/marchpast/issues/133)'s.
- **Nothing else about a strike needs schema.** `position` is its carrier's at launch and recovery and its own in between; `heading` its course, and the attack run over the target (ADR-0019's fighting front); `formation` one of the three words; `strength` the fraction still with the strike, opening strength taken at launch, so VT-8 runs 1 to 0 in twenty minutes; `state` `intact` on deck and en route, `engaged` from the attack, `broken` when it scatters, `destroyed` when nothing comes home.
- **A ship is `broken` while she burns and `destroyed` when she sinks or is scuttled.** Kaga is `broken` at 10:22 and `destroyed` at 19:25; Akagi `broken` at 10:26 and `destroyed` when she is scuttled at 05:00 on the 5th; Yorktown `engaged` when bombed, `broken` when abandoned, `destroyed` at 05:01 on the 7th. This keeps `destroyed` on a dated event that a source attests — every sinking position here is from the Nagumo report — rather than on an undated judgment about fighting capability.
- **Midway's file is two levels, `["Forces", "Ships and strikes"]`**, strikes parented to `mobile-force`, `tf16`, `tf17` and `midway`. `midway-air` is a permanent depth-1 sibling carrying VMF-221's interception as its own state and strength, so the atoll does not flicker on and off the level as its aircraft come and go. `main-body` and `occupation-force` stay off the roster; CruDiv 7 splits into Mogami and Mikuma or does not, as the authoring pass prefers.
- **Midway's map file holds the atoll and nothing else**: Sand and Eastern Islands and Kure as `land`, the reef as `shoal`, a `place` for each atoll and a `work` for the air station Tomonaga was sent to bomb, all from Natural Earth 10m. Licence `public-domain`. `map` stays optional in the schema; this is Midway's answer, not a rule.
