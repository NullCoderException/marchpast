Collapse order when the plate gets crowded
Each step is taken only when the one before still collides. Engaged units and units with a move keep their labels longest; among equals, roster order. Step 5 is the last resort and needs a per-frame numeral key in the legend, so it is a proposal for #39 to prove or reject.
