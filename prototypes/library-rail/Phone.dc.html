Marchpast
Famous battles, played back on the map — phase by phase, from the sources up.
How it is made, and where the battles come from →
  1. The Battle of Cannae 2 August 216 BC Roman · Carthaginian Hannibal lets his centre give ground until the Roman army has walked into a bag, then closes his Libyans and his cavalry round it and annihilates the largest army Rome ever put in the field.
  2. 164 years later
    The Siege of Alesia September 52 BC Gaul · Rome Caesar shuts Vercingetorix inside two rings of ditch and rampart, twenty-one miles of them, and holds both faces at once when a Gallic army four times his size comes down on his back.
  3. 1,849 years later
    The Battle of the Nile 1 August 1798 British · French Nelson finds the French fleet moored across Aboukir Bay, doubles its van at sunset and fights all night; by the second morning only two ships of the line are left to escape.
  4. 2 years later
    The Battle of Copenhagen 2 April 1801 British · Danish Nelson takes twelve of the line up the King's Deep, destroys the Danish line of blockships at anchor, and ends the day under a flag of truce.
  5. 4 years later
    The Battle of Trafalgar 21 October 1805 British · Combined Fleet Nelson's two columns cut Villeneuve's line off Cape Trafalgar and destroy the Combined Fleet in an afternoon.
  6. 70 years later
    The Battle of the Little Bighorn 25 June 1876 7th Cavalry · The village Custer divides the 7th Cavalry and attacks the largest village on the plains from two directions; within an hour his own five companies are surrounded on a ridge above the ford and killed to the last man.
  7. 65 years later
    The Battle of Midway 4 June 1942 Japan · United States Four Japanese carriers steam on an atoll two hundred miles away, are caught with their decks full, and burn within six minutes of each other; the fourth is found and burned that afternoon.
