Famous battles, played back on the map — phase by phase, from the sources up.
How it is made, and where the battles come from →
  1. The Battle of Cannae 2 August 216 BC Hannibal lets his centre give ground until the Roman army has walked into a bag, then closes his Libyans and his cavalry round it and annihilates the largest army Rome ever put in the field.
  2. The Battle of the Nile 1 August 1798 Nelson finds the French fleet moored across Aboukir Bay, doubles its van at sunset and fights all night; by the second morning only two ships of the line are left to escape.
  3. The Battle of Copenhagen 2 April 1801 Nelson takes twelve of the line up the King's Deep, destroys the Danish line of blockships at anchor, and ends the day under a flag of truce.
  4. The Battle of Trafalgar 21 October 1805 Nelson's two columns cut Villeneuve's line off Cape Trafalgar and destroy the Combined Fleet in an afternoon.
